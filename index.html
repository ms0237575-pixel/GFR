A Letter to You, on Your Twentieth

My dearest Rawan,

Twenty. T-W-E-N-T-Y. Can you believe it? I still remember you like it was yesterday — doing the most, being extra dramatic about the smallest things (lovingly, of course), and somehow still managing to be the most wonderful human in every room you walked into.

You didn't just grow up. You bloomed. And watching you step into your twenties feels like watching the universe finally make good on a very old, very beautiful promise.

You are going to do extraordinary things this decade. Not because life will be easy — it won't always be — but because you carry something rare inside you: a softness that doesn't break, and a fire that doesn't burn out. That combination? It's unstoppable.

Your twenties are for messing up gloriously and learning loudly. They're for choosing yourself first, finding your people, and building a life that looks like you — not anyone else's version of you. I hope you fill every single year with things that make your eyes light up.

And on the days it gets hard — because it will — I hope you remember that you are not alone. You have someone in your corner who chose you, the way sisters choose each other: completely, stubbornly, and forever. That's me. Hi. I'm not going anywhere.

So here's to twenty. Here's to you. Here's to the whole wild, starlit galaxy that's waiting for you. Go light it up, habibi.

With every star in every sky,

Your chosen sibling 🌙
forever & always
